# Theming and behaviour

Everything on the Widget page applies the moment you save it. Installed widgets pick the change up on their next load.

## Appearance

- **Colour scheme:** Auto follows the reader's system preference. Light and Dark force one.
- **Accent colour:** the launcher, the send button and the reader's own messages. Pick from the presets or enter any hex colour. Custom accents are available on Starter and Growth; Hobby uses Parbot amber.
- **Position:** bottom right or bottom left. Applies to the bubble launcher and the palette pill.
- **Corner radius:** small, medium or large.

## Conversation

- **Welcome message:** the first line readers see. Keep it short and say what the assistant covers.
- **Suggested questions:** up to four chips shown before the first question. Good suggestions are the questions your support inbox sees most.
- **Instructions:** on the assistant's Settings page. Extra guidance appended to the assistant's system prompt, for example the product name, the tone, or "always answer in English". Instructions cannot make the assistant answer from outside your docs.

## When the docs fall short

If no passage in your docs covers a question, the assistant says so. With **lead capture** on (Starter and Growth), the widget then offers a small form: an email address and an optional message. Leads appear on the Inbox page with a link to the conversation. Without lead capture the reader simply sees the honest answer, and the question still lands in your unanswered list.

## Branding

Widgets show a small "Powered by Parbot" line. Starter and Growth can turn it off.
