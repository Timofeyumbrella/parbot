## How limits work

- **Assistants** and **indexed pages** are counted across your whole account.
- **Answers** count every reply the assistant gives, in the widget and in the chat, and reset on the first day of each month. When the monthly limit is reached the widget tells readers to come back later and the chat shows the same notice.
- Moving up a plan lifts the limits immediately. Moving down keeps what you have; you will not be able to add more until you are under the new limits.

## Paying

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Choose a plan on the Billing page, complete the checkout, and the plan is applied within a few seconds. "Manage subscription" opens Stripe's portal, where you can change the card, download invoices, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then drops to Hobby.
