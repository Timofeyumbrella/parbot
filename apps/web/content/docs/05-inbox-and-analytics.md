# Inbox and analytics

Every conversation, from the widget and from the in-app chat, is kept and readable.

## Overview

The assistant's Overview is built around what to fix next. It opens on the last 7 days, compared with the 7 days before; switch to 30 days at the top for the longer trend. Every section counts the period shown. An assistant that has not answered anything yet shows how to get its first questions instead.

- **Answer quality.** The answer rate (answers that came from the docs, out of every answer the assistant finished), how helpful readers found them (thumbs up out of every rated answer, with the number of ratings, so a handful reads as the small sample it is), and the median time from a question to the finished answer. Each shows its change on the period of the same length before, with an arrow: green when it moved the right way, red when it did not. An answer the reader stopped is left out of the rate and the time. Below the numbers, a small daily chart of answered against unanswered questions, with a table view.
- **Knowledge gaps.** The questions the docs could not answer, with different wordings of the same question grouped together ("Is there a Slack integration?" and "Do you have a Slack integration" count as one). Each shows how often it was asked, when it was last asked, and links to that conversation. **Add docs** opens the Add source dialog in Knowledge. Once the docs cover a question, re-index the source and ask it in Chat to check.
- **Answers readers disliked.** The latest answers rated thumbs down, with the question and the answer's first line. Each links straight to that answer in its transcript. A thumbs down usually means the page behind the answer is wrong, out of date or unclear.
- **Content that works, and content that does not.** The pages cited most in answers, with how many answers used each, and the indexed pages no answer cited in the period, out of all of them. Pages that are never cited may be hard to find, duplicated or out of date. A page that was re-indexed keeps the citations it had before.
- **Where readers ask.** The pages of your docs site the widget was used on, by path, with how many questions came from each and the answer rate there. A low answer rate on a page means its topic is thin in the docs. This fills once the widget is on your site.
- **Usage against the plan.** Answers used on your account this month against the plan's monthly allowance, and where the month ends at the current pace ("at this pace you will use about 2,340 of 3,000"). When the pace runs past the limit, it says around which day and links to Billing.
- **Leads.** How many readers left an email in the period, how many are still marked new, and a link to the Leads tab.

The page follows new conversations, ratings and leads while it is open.

## Inbox

The Inbox lists conversations newest first. Filter by channel (widget or in-app) or show only conversations with unanswered questions. Widget conversations show the page the reader was on. New conversations appear live while the page is open.

Open a conversation to read the transcript with its citations. Readers can rate an answer with a thumb up or down in the widget and in the chat; the rating shows in the transcript.

## Leads

The Leads tab lists every email left through the widget, with the reader's note, the page, and a status you can move from new to contacted to closed.

## Retention

Conversations are kept for as long as the assistant exists. Deleting a conversation removes its messages. Deleting your assistant in Settings removes everything it owns: sources, pages, conversations and leads. You then create a new one from scratch.
