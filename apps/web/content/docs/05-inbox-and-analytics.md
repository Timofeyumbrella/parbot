# Inbox and analytics

Every conversation, from the widget and from the in-app chat, is kept and readable.

## Overview

The assistant's Overview shows, for the last 7 or 30 days: conversations, questions, the answer rate, leads, and a daily chart of answered against unanswered questions. Below it, the top questions and the unanswered ones. Unanswered questions are the most useful list on the page: each one is a page your docs are missing, and each links to the conversation it came from.

## Inbox

The Inbox lists conversations newest first. Filter by channel (widget or in-app) or show only conversations with unanswered questions. Widget conversations show the page the reader was on. New conversations appear live while the page is open.

Open a conversation to read the transcript with its citations. Readers can rate an answer with a thumb up or down in the widget and in the chat; the rating shows in the transcript.

## Leads

The Leads tab lists every email left through the widget, with the reader's note, the page, and a status you can move from new to contacted to closed.

## Retention

Conversations are kept for as long as the assistant exists. Deleting a conversation removes its messages. Deleting an assistant removes everything it owns: sources, pages, conversations and leads.
